## 3. Reporting, Recusal, and Escalation Instructions

I understand and agree that:

1. Ongoing Duty to Disclose
- I will promptly**update**this disclosure in writing if, during 2026, new circumstances arise or I become aware of information that creates, or may reasonably appear to create, a conflict of interest relating to the Organization.
2. Primary Reporting Channel
- I will promptly report any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest to**The Organization’s Executive Director (ED)**or the ED’s authorized designee, in accordance with the Conflict of Interest Policy and any related procedures.
3. If the Executive Director Is Involved
- If the actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest**involves the ED**, I will report the matter**directly to the the Organization Board President**(or the Board President’s authorized designee) instead of the ED.
- I understand that the Board President (or designee) will follow the procedures in the Conflict of Interest Policy, including any required committee review, recusal from discussion or voting, and documentation of the decision.
4. Meeting Participation and Recusal
- When a matter in which I have a disclosed conflict (or potential conflict) is being discussed or voted on, I will follow the**recusal**and**non‑participation**requirements in the Conflict of Interest Policy, including leaving the meeting during deliberation and voting if directed to do so.

## 4. Confidentiality and Legal / Whistleblower Carve‑Out

I acknowledge and agree that:

1. I will maintain the**confidentiality**of the Organization’s non‑public information, including board and committee materials, personnel and compensation information, donor and funder information, non‑public financial data, and other Confidential Information, as defined in the Conflict of Interest Policy.
2. I will use the Organization’s Confidential Information**solely**for legitimate the Organization purposes and not for personal gain or the benefit of any third party.

However, I further understand and agree that:

1. Nothing in this form or in the Organization’s Conflict of Interest Policy is intended to, or will be interpreted to, prohibit or restrict me from:
- Communicating with, filing a charge or complaint with, or otherwise reporting possible violations of law or regulation to any**governmental, regulatory, or oversight agency or entity**, including but not limited to federal or state agencies;
- Making disclosures that are**protected under whistleblower laws**, including the right to report in good faith suspected fraud, waste, abuse, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or other misconduct; or
- Complying with any**lawful subpoena, court order, or other legal requirement**to disclose information.
2. I understand that I do not need prior authorization from the Organization, and I am not required to notify the Organization, before making any disclosure that is expressly protected by applicable whistleblower or other laws.
