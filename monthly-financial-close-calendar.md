# Monthly financial close calendar

A one-page schedule the bookkeeper, the Executive Director, and the Treasurer all work from. Three dates a month, every month, published a year ahead so nobody has to ask when the numbers are coming.

| Month | Books closed | Analysis complete | Reports to board |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| February | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| March | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| April | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |

| July | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| August | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| September | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| October | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| November | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |
| December | 2nd Tuesday | close + 7 days | close + 9 or 10 days |

What each date means:

- **Books closed.** All transactions for the prior month are entered and reconciled: bank and credit card accounts, payment processor payouts, payroll, and any grant receivables. After this date the prior month is not edited without a note explaining why.
- **Analysis complete.** Budget versus actual by program and by function, cash position and runway, restricted versus unrestricted balances, and a short written commentary on anything more than ten percent off budget.
- **Reports to board.** The package goes to the Treasurer and the board on the Friday of that week, before the following board or finance committee meeting, so questions come in writing first.

Replace the pattern with real dates once a year and send the calendar to everyone who touches the books.
