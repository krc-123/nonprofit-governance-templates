# Nonprofit governance templates

Working governance documents from a national nonprofit I run as Executive Director, generalized so another small or mid-sized organization can adopt them. I drafted them during a board and policy rebuild in my first months in the role, and they are written for an organization with a remote staff, contractors, volunteers, and a board that meets a few times a year. Have your own counsel read anything you adopt.

Every file is Markdown so it can be read on GitHub, diffed when it changes, and pasted into a document for signature. Bracketed fields such as `[Organization Name]`, `[State]`, and `[Effective date]` are yours to fill. Dollar thresholds and timelines are the ones we use; treat them as a starting point, not advice.

## Board

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `whistleblower-policy-board.md` | How directors and officers raise concerns, who investigates, and the no-retaliation rule. Required by IRS Form 990 Part VI |
| `conflict-of-interest-and-confidentiality-policy.md` | The organization-wide policy in article form, with definitions, disclosure, recusal, and the annual acknowledgment |
| `conflict-of-interest-annual-disclosure-form.md` | The form every director, officer, and key staff member signs each year |
| `board-nda-and-confidentiality-agreement.md` | What board members may and may not share, written so it does not cut across their fiduciary duties |
| `executive-director-scope-and-escalation-board-guide.md` | The board-facing half of the delegation framework: reserved powers, approval thresholds, a RACI, reporting cadence, and emergency escalation |

## Staff and contractors

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `whistleblower-policy-staff-and-contractors.md` | The staff version of the whistleblower policy, in plain language |
| `staff-conflict-of-interest-policy.md` | Outside work, vendor relationships, gifts, and family ties, for employees and long-term contractors |
| `staff-nda-and-confidentiality-agreement.md` | Confidentiality for staff and contractors, including participant data and donor information |
| `staff-code-of-conduct-and-ed-scope-guide.md` | The staff-facing half of the delegation framework: what the Executive Director can decide alone, what needs an officer or the board, spending and contracting limits, hiring rules, and escalation paths with timelines and templates |
| `interview-question-bank-and-hiring-guidance.md` | Structured, job-related interview questions, the questions US employers must not ask, and how to handle accommodation requests during hiring |

## Operations

| File | Use it for |

## Notes on using them

The two scope and escalation guides are a pair. The board guide sets the rules; the staff guide translates them into what a program manager actually does on a Tuesday. Adopt both or neither.

The whistleblower and conflict of interest policies exist in board and staff versions on purpose. Directors have fiduciary duties and staff have employment relationships, and one document that tries to cover both tends to be read by nobody.

Check state law before adopting. Nonprofit corporation statutes differ on director duties, on what a board may delegate, and on whistleblower protections. These documents were drafted for a corporation organized in one US state and have not been reviewed for yours.
