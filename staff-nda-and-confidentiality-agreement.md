# the Organization Staff NDA & Confidentiality Agreement (2026 Draft)

**[Organization Name] Staff Non‑Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement**

## 1. Purpose, Scope, and Parties

This Staff Non‑Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between:

- **[Organization Name]**, a [State] nonprofit corporation (“the Organization”), and
- The individual identified in the signature block below (“Staff Member”).

For purposes of this Agreement, “Staff Member” includes any the Organization:

- Employee (full‑time, part‑time, temporary, or seasonal)
- Independent contractor or consultant
- Intern, fellow, or paid apprentice
- Volunteer acting in a staff‑like capacity with access to non‑public information

This Agreement is intended to:

- Protect the confidentiality, privacy, and security of the Organization information and of the children, families, members, donors, employees, contractors, and partners we serve
- Establish clear rules for permissible use and disclosure of Confidential Information
- Address security expectations for remote and hybrid work
- Clarify ownership of intellectual property and work product
- Describe what obligations continue after a Staff Member’s relationship with the Organization ends
- Preserve rights to report concerns to law enforcement, regulators, and other appropriate authorities

This Agreement applies in addition to, and not in place of, the the Organization Bylaws, Staff Code of Conduct, Conflict of Interest Policy, Employment Agreement (if any), and any Board‑approved policies or written contracts that apply to the Staff Member’s role.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 “Confidential Information”

“**Confidential Information**” means any non‑public information, in any form (oral, written, electronic, audio, video, or visual), that is disclosed or made available to the Staff Member in connection with the Organization work, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, including but not limited to:

1. Community, Participant, and Family Information
- Names and contact information of program participants, parents, caregivers, and family members
- Registration data, participation history, and attendance records for classes, webinars, events, and programs
- Application materials, surveys, feedback forms, case notes, or other information related to individuals’ needs, accommodations, communication preferences, or family situations
- Media (photos, audio, video, chat logs) or stories involving children, youth, or families, whether or not consent has been obtained for particular uses
2. Member, Donor, and Supporter Information
- Membership lists and member contact details, preferences, and engagement history
- Donor and prospect lists; giving history; capacity, interest, and strategy notes
- Sponsorship, partnership, and grant prospect information; cultivation and solicitation plans
- Data from the Organization’s constituent relationship management (CRM) or similar systems
3. Personnel and HR Information
- Information about employees, contractors, interns, and volunteers, including applications, performance evaluations, compensation, disciplinary actions, schedules, background checks, and accommodations
- Internal communications about HR, safety, or conduct concerns and investigations
4. Program, Curriculum, and Operational Information
- Program content, curricula, lesson plans, recorded classes or webinars, and evaluation results not publicly released
- Internal procedures, checklists, manuals, planning documents, vendor lists, and non‑public program evaluations
- Internal calendars, workplans, and non‑public strategies related to programs, outreach, and services
5. Financial and Business Information
- Non‑public budgets, forecasts, dashboards, internal financial reports, payroll and benefits information
- Information relating to grants, contracts, sponsorships, and other funding arrangements, including terms, rates, and performance requirements
- Bank and investment account details, internal controls, and audit findings
6. Technology, Security, and Systems Information
- Non‑public details of the Organization information systems, architecture, integrations, security configurations, and risk assessments
- Login credentials, API keys, tokens, multi‑factor authentication (MFA) details, access control configurations, and internal URLs
- Incident reports and vulnerabilities relating to the Organization data or systems
7. Governance and Internal Deliberations
- Draft policies, internal memoranda, and non‑public Board or committee materials shared with staff
- Non‑public deliberations regarding strategy, risk, personnel, litigation, or reputation
8. Third‑Party and Regulated Information
- Non‑public information provided to the Organization by funders, schools, partners, or vendors under confidentiality or data‑use terms
- Any personal information that is protected by applicable privacy, data‑protection, or child‑serving laws or regulations

Confidential Information includes any non‑public combinations, analyses, summaries, and derivatives of such information created or accessed by the Staff Member.

### 2.2 Excluded Information

Information is**not**Confidential Information under this Agreement to the extent the Staff Member can demonstrate, with documentation, that:

1. The information was or becomes**publicly available**through no breach of this Agreement or other duty to the Organization;
2. The information was**lawfully known**to the Staff Member, without confidentiality obligation, before the Organization disclosed it;
3. The information is**independently developed**by the Staff Member without reference to or use of the Organization Confidential Information; or
4. The information is received in good faith from another source that has the**legal right**to disclose it without confidentiality obligation.

The existence of one of these exclusions for a specific piece of information does not authorize broader disclosure of other related Confidential Information.

## 3. Confidentiality Obligations

### 3.1 Core Duty

The Staff Member shall:

1. **Use**Confidential Information solely for legitimate the Organization business purposes and only as reasonably necessary to perform assigned duties for the Organization; and
2. **Not disclose**Confidential Information to any person or entity, inside or outside the Organization, except as expressly permitted in this Agreement or by written authorization from the Executive Director (or designee with appropriate authority).

### 3.2 Need‑to‑Know and Minimum Necessary

The Staff Member shall:

- Access, use, and share Confidential Information only with individuals (staff, contractors, volunteers, or board members) who:
- Have authorized the Organization roles that reasonably require such information; and
- Have agreed to comparable confidentiality obligations;
- Limit the amount and detail of Confidential Information shared to the**minimum necessary**for the specific purpose.

### 3.3 Restrictions on Personal Use

The Staff Member shall**not**:

- Use Confidential Information for personal benefit or for the benefit of any third party, including future employers or organizations;
- Save or store Confidential Information in personal systems or accounts (e.g., personal email, personal cloud storage, personal devices) except as explicitly authorized;
- Use Confidential Information for outside consulting or volunteer work unrelated to the Organization, even if mission‑aligned, without written authorization.

## 4. Security and Remote Work Requirements

The Organization’s operations are largely remote. As a condition of remote or hybrid work, the Staff Member agrees to the following security practices, in addition to any the Organization IT or data‑security policies:

### 4.1 Devices and Accounts

1. Approved Devices
- Use only the Organization‑provided or the Organization‑approved devices to access systems containing Confidential Information.
- Maintain up‑to‑date operating systems, antivirus/anti‑malware tools, and security patches on all such devices.
2. Access Controls
- Protect devices with strong passwords, PINs, or biometrics and enable automatic screen lock after a short period of inactivity.
- Use strong, unique passwords for the Organization systems and enable multi‑factor authentication (MFA) wherever available.
- Do not share user accounts or passwords with any person, including family members, colleagues, or supervisors.
- Immediately report any suspected compromise of credentials or devices to the Executive Director or designated IT/security contact.

### 4.2 Networks and Storage

1. Secure Networks
- Avoid accessing Confidential Information over unsecured public Wi‑Fi unless using an the Organization‑approved VPN.
- Do not configure “remember me” or auto‑login on shared or public devices.
2. Storage and Transmission
- Use only the Organization‑approved cloud storage, email, CRM, and collaboration platforms for Confidential Information.
- Encrypt, or use encrypted platforms for, transmitting any highly sensitive information (such as detailed participant records, financial account credentials, or HR files).
- Do not store Confidential Information on unencrypted USB drives, local downloads, or personal external media.

### 4.3 Physical Safeguards

1. Workspace
- Maintain a workspace that protects Confidential Information from being viewed or overheard by unauthorized persons.
- Do not leave printed materials or unlocked devices where others can access them.
2. Printed Materials
- Only print Confidential Information when necessary; store printouts securely; and destroy them using a shredder or secure disposal when no longer needed.
- Do not discard Confidential Information in regular trash or recycling.

## 5. Privacy and Safeguarding for Children, Families, and Community Members

Because the Organization serves children and families, the Staff Member agrees to additional safeguards:

1. Limit Disclosure of Identifying Details
- Do not share names, images, video, or identifying stories about children, youth, or families outside of the Organization without appropriate consent and compliance with the Organization policies.
- Use de‑identified or aggregated data whenever possible in reports, presentations, and public communications.
2. Use of Media and Recordings
- Follow the Organization consent and media policies before capturing or sharing images, audio, video, or chat logs involving children or families.
- Do not download, clip, or repurpose recorded sessions or screenshots for personal use or non‑the Organization projects.
3. Special Categories of Data
- Treat information about disability, language use, educational placements, or other sensitive characteristics as especially confidential and share only on a strict need‑to‑know basis.

## 6. Permitted Disclosures

The Staff Member may disclose Confidential Information**only**in the following circumstances:

1. Within the Organization, on a Need‑to‑Know Basis
- To the Organization employees, contractors, volunteers, officers, or directors who reasonably require the information to perform their responsibilities and who are subject to comparable confidentiality obligations.
2. To Authorized Third Parties
- To funders, schools, partner organizations, or vendors, but only:
- When expressly authorized by the Executive Director (or designee);
- In accordance with the Organization agreements, consent forms, and policies; and
- With appropriate safeguards and minimum necessary detail.
3. As Required by Law
- As further described in Section 8 (Whistleblower and Legal Carveouts), when disclosure is required or protected by law.

## 7. Return, Deletion, and Destruction of Information

### 7.1 During Engagement

Upon request by the Executive Director or the Organization’s authorized representative, the Staff Member shall promptly:

- Return or securely transmit specified Confidential Information to the Organization;
- Delete specified Confidential Information from personal devices or accounts, if any, consistent with any instructions provided; and
- Provide written confirmation that such deletion and return have occurred, subject to any legal preservation duties.

### 7.2 At End of Relationship

Upon the conclusion of the Staff Member’s employment, contract, or other engagement with the Organization (for any reason):

1. The Staff Member shall, within a reasonable period not to exceed ten (10) business days (unless otherwise directed in writing):
- Return to the Organization all devices, documents, files, notes, media, and other materials (including copies and backups) containing Confidential Information that are in the Staff Member’s possession or control, regardless of format;
- Permanently delete or destroy all other copies of Confidential Information that cannot practicably be returned, using secure deletion methods appropriate to the medium; and
- Cease all access to the Organization systems and accounts.
2. The Staff Member may retain:
- Personal contact information for colleagues and professional references (provided it does not include Confidential Information about the Organization constituents or internal operations); and
- Copies of this Agreement and any other agreements signed by the Staff Member.

The obligations in this Section 7 do not require the Staff Member to delete or destroy information that must be retained to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or directive from a government agency, provided that the Staff Member continues to protect such information as Confidential Information and, where lawful, notifies the Organization of the need for retention.

## 8. Whistleblower and Legal Carveouts; Protected Rights

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to, or shall be interpreted to, prevent, interfere with, or discourage the Staff Member from engaging in any of the activities listed below. To the extent any provision of this Agreement conflicts with this Section 8,**this Section 8 controls**.

### 8.1 Reporting to Government, Law Enforcement, and Regulators

The Staff Member may,**without notice to the Organization**, and without violating this Agreement:

- Report possible violations of law, regulations, or ethical rules to any governmental, regulatory, or law enforcement agency or entity (including but not limited to child‑protective services, civil rights agencies, the U.S. Department of Education, the U.S. Department of Labor, the Internal Revenue Service, state charity regulators, and state or federal prosecutors);
- Cooperate with any investigation, proceeding, hearing, or inquiry by such agencies;
- Provide documents or other information requested by such agencies, subject to lawful process.

### 8.2 Reporting Safety, Abuse, or Misconduct Concerns

The Staff Member may disclose Confidential Information, consistent with applicable law, to:

- Report suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, or significant safety concerns involving children, families, or community members;
- Report discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or other workplace misconduct;
- Seek advice from the Staff Member’s own legal counsel, provided the Staff Member takes reasonable steps to preserve confidentiality where appropriate.

### 8.3 Protected Concerted Activity and Other Rights

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to limit any rights the Staff Member may have under applicable labor or employment laws, including:

- Discussing wages, hours, or working conditions with coworkers;
- Engaging in concerted activity regarding terms and conditions of employment;
- Filing a charge or complaint with, or participating in an investigation by, the National Labor Relations Board, Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, or similar agencies.

The Staff Member remains responsible for protecting**Confidential Information of the Organization’s community members, donors, and other non‑staff**, except to the extent disclosure is required or protected under Sections 8.1 and 8.2.

### 8.4 Response to Subpoenas or Court Orders

If the Staff Member receives a subpoena, court order, or similar legal process requesting the Organization Confidential Information and is**not**prohibited by law or order from doing so, the Staff Member should:

- Promptly notify the Organization’s Executive Director or Board President; and
- Provide a copy of the subpoena or order, so that the Organization may seek appropriate protections (such as a protective order).

## 9. Intellectual Property and Work Product

### 9.1 the Organization Ownership of Work Product

To the fullest extent permitted by law:

1. Employees – Work Made for Hire
- All work product created or developed by the Staff Member within the scope of their employment with the Organization—including but not limited to curricula, lesson plans, presentations, written materials, designs, media content, reports, data models, templates, and software code—is considered a “work made for hire” owned exclusively by the Organization.
2. Contractors and Other Staff
- To the extent any work product created by a contractor, consultant, or other non‑employee is not a “work made for hire,” the Staff Member hereby irrevocably assigns and agrees to assign to the Organization all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to such work product, including all intellectual property rights.

### 9.2 Pre‑Existing Materials; Tools and Know‑How

1. Pre‑Existing Materials
- The Staff Member retains ownership of materials created before their engagement with the Organization (e.g., generic training templates, personal frameworks) that are clearly separable from the Organization‑specific content.
- If any such pre‑existing materials are incorporated into the Organization work product, the Staff Member grants the Organization a perpetual, worldwide, royalty‑free, irrevocable license to use, reproduce, adapt, and distribute those elements as integrated into the the Organization work product.
2. General Skills and Experience
- Nothing in this Agreement prevents the Staff Member from using general skills, knowledge, or experience gained while working with the Organization, provided that they do not use or disclose the Organization Confidential Information, donor lists, non‑public strategies, or other protected information in doing so.

### 9.3 Use of Third‑Party Intellectual Property

The Staff Member agrees to:

- Use third‑party content, software, and platforms (including stock media, fonts, and tools) only in accordance with applicable licenses and the Organization policies;
- Not upload or share the Organization Confidential Information with tools or platforms that are not approved by the Organization for such use; and
- Promptly notify the Organization if they become aware of any actual or potential intellectual property infringement related to their the Organization work.

## 10. Term, Survival, and Remedies

### 10.1 Term of Agreement

This Agreement becomes effective on the date signed by the Staff Member and continues:

- For the duration of the Staff Member’s employment, contract, or engagement with the Organization; and
- **After**the conclusion of such relationship, as specified below.

### 10.2 Survival of Obligations

The following obligations survive termination of the Staff Member’s relationship with the Organization, for so long as the information at issue remains non‑public:

- Confidentiality and non‑disclosure obligations under Sections 2–7;
- Whistleblower and legal carveouts under Section 8;
- Intellectual property and work‑product provisions under Section 9; and
- Cooperation with the Organization regarding return or destruction of Confidential Information under Section 7.

### 10.3 Remedies

The Staff Member acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure or misuse of Confidential Information, or misappropriation of the Organization work product, may cause the Organization and its community irreparable harm.

Accordingly, in the event of an actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, and subject to applicable law:

- the Organization may pursue any remedies available at law or in equity, including injunctive or other equitable relief, without waiving any other rights or remedies; and
- The Staff Member may be subject to corrective action, up to and including termination of employment or engagement, in accordance with applicable policies and contracts.

Nothing in this Section 10.3 limits the Staff Member’s rights under Section 8 (Whistleblower and Legal Carveouts).

## 11. Relationship to Other the Organization Policies and Agreements

- This Agreement is intended to complement, and does not limit or replace, the the Organization Bylaws, Staff Code of Conduct, Conflict of Interest Policy, Staff Conflict of Interest Policy, Employment Agreement, or any other Board‑approved or the Organization‑approved policies and contracts.
- If there is any conflict between this Agreement and a Board‑approved Employment Agreement or other written contract signed by the Organization and the Staff Member, the specific contract language will govern that subject, and this Agreement will be construed as consistent with that contract to the greatest extent possible.
- If there is any conflict between this Agreement and the Organization’s policies regarding whistleblowing, reporting of concerns, or cooperation with regulators or law enforcement, this Agreement shall be interpreted to**preserve**those reporting and cooperation rights.

## 12. Governing Law and Forum

This Agreement, and any dispute arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the**[State of Incorporation]**, without regard to its conflict‑of‑laws rules.

Unless prohibited by applicable law, any legal action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought in a state or federal court located in:

- The [State of Incorporation],**or**
- The state in which the Organization maintains its principal office at the time of the dispute,

and the parties consent to the personal jurisdiction and venue of such courts for such purposes.
